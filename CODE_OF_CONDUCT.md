# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance,
race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Behaviour that helps:

- Being kind, and assuming good faith
- Respecting differing viewpoints and experience
- Giving and accepting constructive feedback gracefully
- Owning our mistakes, apologising, and learning from them
- Prioritising what is best for the community over what is best for us

Behaviour that does not:

- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention
- Trolling, insults, derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission
- Anything else a reasonable person would consider inappropriate in a
  professional setting

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to **conduct@openbox.ai**. Reports are reviewed
promptly and in confidence, and we will respect the reporter's privacy and
safety. Maintainers are responsible for clarifying these standards and may take
appropriate corrective action — from a private warning to a permanent ban —
in response to behaviour they consider inappropriate.
